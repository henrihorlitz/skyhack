Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Use teal for the one main action on a screen.
  • Keep cards white, sitting on the mint canvas.
  • Use pills for buttons, status, and search.
  • Save coral for something the family must not hear yet.

Don't

  • Don't add a third bright color.
  • Don't use sharp corners on buttons or cards.
  • Don't put medical jargon on the family timeline.
  • Don't invent a discharge date if the note has none.

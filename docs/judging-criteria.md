# Judging criteria

Source: kickoff slide "How you win: Ideas to impact", SKYHACK 2026.
"We're looking for bold ideas, strong execution, and real-world impact."

| Criterion | What the slide says | What it means for MindPeace |
|---|---|---|
| **Pitch** | How clearly you communicate the idea and vision. | A real story (nurse in Lisbon, the 12–13h window), one clear problem, and a vision beyond the demo. |
| **Technical Execution** | How well it works and is built. | The demo path runs live end to end: the note → filtered briefing, the approval, the voice agent with real tool calls. Fallbacks, so nothing crashes. |
| **Impact** | The potential to solve a real problem. | Ward example: 22 families, 3 doctors, 1 phone hour. Doctors save time, families get certainty, nurses get fewer calls they aren't allowed to answer. |
| **Innovation** | How original and creative the approach is. | The AI knows what *not* to say (built on the real nurse vs. doctor boundary), and unanswered questions come back to the doctor as one queue. |
| **User Experience** | How intuitive, useful and delightful it is. | A family app a stressed 60-year-old can read in 5 seconds, a doctor approval in one click, and a voice for people who can't use an app. |

Weighting was not shown, so assume all five are equal.

# MindPeace: 1-minute elevator pitch

~140 words ≈ 60 seconds. Bold = the lines that must land.

---

**My girlfriend is a nurse on a hospital ward in Lisbon.**
22 patients, 3 doctors, and one phone hour a day, from 12 to 1.
Families call again and again, and nobody picks up.
And she's legally allowed to tell a worried daughter one thing: *"She's stable."*

**MindPeace fixes that.**
Our AI reads the doctor's daily note and turns it into a plain-language update for the family.
It follows the rules hospitals already follow: what a nurse may say is shared, medical details need the doctor's approval, and a diagnosis the patient hasn't heard yet stays with the doctor.
**The doctor approves it with one click.**

The family gets a push notification, a clear timeline, and a voice assistant they can call any time.
Questions it can't answer go straight to the doctor, with a callback booked.

**Free for families. Hospitals pay:** their doctors get the phone hour back, and families choose the hospital that keeps them informed.

**One click for the doctor. Peace of mind for the family.**
